TASK_VALIDITY classification guidance (not difficulty evidence):
Classify the real-life setting, not an incidental action or medium. Office meetings, workplace reports, project handovers and employee deadlines are WORK, even when scheduling or software is involved; do not relabel them SCHEDULE or DIGITAL_LIFE.
