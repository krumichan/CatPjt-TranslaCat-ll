Classify the real-life setting, not an incidental action or medium. Office meetings, workplace reports, project handovers and employee deadlines are WORK, even when scheduling or software is involved; do not relabel them SCHEDULE or DIGITAL_LIFE. Travel plans are TRAVEL, buying/returning goods is SHOPPING, meals are FOOD, study is LEARNING, leisure is HOBBY, personal relationships are SOCIAL, ordinary home life is DAILY_LIFE. SERVICE is consumer/service interaction, DIGITAL_LIFE is personal digital activity, HEALTH_GENERAL is non-specialist everyday health. Use SCHEDULE for genuinely general timing without a more specific setting. Respect legitimate context ambiguity; classify by the main setting.
